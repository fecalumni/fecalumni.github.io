About the Alumni Association

Strengthening the lifelong bond between Faridpur Engineering College and its graduates.

Our Mission

The FEC Alumni Association serves as the official bridge between Faridpur Engineering College and its graduates. We foster a vibrant, supportive community where alumni can reconnect, mentor current students, share professional opportunities, and contribute to the continued growth of the institution that shaped their careers.

What We Do

  • Maintain a verified alumni directory
  • Organize reunions and networking events
  • Facilitate mentorship for current students
  • Share career and collaboration opportunities
  • Keep alumni informed about institutional developments

Who Can Join

All graduates of Faridpur Engineering College are invited to join. Membership is verified through an administrator approval process to ensure the directory remains authentic and trustworthy.

About Faridpur Engineering College

Faridpur Engineering College (FEC) is a public engineering college affiliated with the University of Dhaka, located in Faridpur, Bangladesh. The college offers undergraduate programs in Civil Engineering, Electrical and Electronic Engineering, and Computer Science and Engineering has been serving the region with quality engineering education.

This alumni portal is an independent initiative to connect FEC graduates worldwide.
